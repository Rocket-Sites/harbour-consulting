How a job runs

Four steps, in this order, every time. At the end of each one you are told what happens next and what it will cost.

  1. We come and measure it

    Or we work from your drawing if you have one. There is no charge for this part and nothing is owed afterwards if you decide not to go ahead.

  2. You get it in writing

    One page, itemised, with the lead time on it. Nothing is ordered and no metal is cut until you have said yes to that page.

  3. It is made here

    In our own workshop, by the people who quoted it. You get a photograph at the point where changing your mind is still cheap.

  4. We fit it and clean up

    Then we walk you around it before we go. Anything not right on the day is put right on the day.

What we take on

Three kinds of work. Most jobs are one of the first two, and the third is what we do between them.

Fabrication

One-off pieces made to a drawing, or to a measurement we take ourselves. Stairs, balustrades, gates, frames, brackets, and the awkward part nobody else wanted to quote on.

Quoted per job

Repairs and alterations

Something that has bent, cracked, rusted or stopped closing properly. We will tell you plainly when a repair is worth less than a replacement, including when that costs us the work.

Usually within the week

Site work

Cutting, welding and installation at your place rather than ours, for work that cannot come to the workshop or should not be moved twice.

Booked by the day

The workshop

A small shop that would rather do a few jobs properly than many of them quickly. Everything is made on the premises, so the person who takes the measurement is the person who cuts to it, and there is nobody to pass the blame to when it is out. We say no to work we are not set up for, and we say it early, which is usually the most useful thing we can do for you.

Questions we get asked first

If yours is not here, call and ask it. The answer is usually shorter than the question.

Do you charge to come and quote?

Not for the visit or the quote. If you need drawings prepared before there is anything to quote on, that is separate work and we will say so before doing any of it.

How far ahead are you booked?

It moves with the season, so the honest answer is on the quote rather than on this page. Repairs are normally fitted in sooner than fabrication.

Will you work from my drawing?

Yes, and we will check it against the opening before anything is cut. If the drawing and the building disagree, you hear about it that day rather than on delivery day.

What if it is not right?

Tell us and we come back. Our own mistakes are put right at our cost, and we would rather hear about one early than find out from somebody else later.
